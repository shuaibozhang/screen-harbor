# ScreenHarbor 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-09-08

## 中文

ScreenHarbor `0.2.0-beta.1` 是一款本地运行的 Windows 录屏工具。

- 不要求创建账户或登录。
- 不包含广告、分析、遥测或崩溃上报服务。
- 不会主动把屏幕画面、麦克风音频、系统声音、设备名称、文件路径或使用情况传输到 ScreenHarbor 开发者或第三方服务器。
- 录制文件、预览、封面、应用设置和录制库索引均保存在用户计算机上。
- 用户可以通过应用删除录制，或使用 Windows 文件管理器自行管理这些文件。
- 安装包包含 FFmpeg 运行时，但应用运行期间不会为 FFmpeg 下载或上传媒体。

操作系统、下载平台和支付平台可能按照各自的隐私政策处理下载、支付或安全检查数据，这些处理不由 ScreenHarbor 控制。

如果未来版本引入联网更新、错误报告或其他数据处理，本政策会在发布前更新，并在需要时征得用户同意。

## English

ScreenHarbor `0.2.0-beta.1` is a locally running screen recorder for Windows.

- No account or sign-in is required.
- The app contains no advertising, analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting service.
- It does not intentionally transmit screen content, microphone audio, system audio, device names, file paths, or usage information to the ScreenHarbor developer or third-party servers.
- Recordings, previews, covers, settings, and the recording-library index remain on the user's computer.
- Users can delete recordings through the app or manage the files directly with Windows File Explorer.
- The installer includes an FFmpeg runtime, but the app does not use FFmpeg to upload or download media at runtime.

The operating system, download provider, and payment provider may process download, payment, or security-check data under their own privacy policies. ScreenHarbor does not control that processing.

If a future release introduces online updates, error reporting, or other data processing, this policy will be updated before release and consent will be requested where required.
